## Overview

A comprehensive business analysis and intrinsic valuation of Walmart Inc. (NYSE: WMT), covering macroeconomic context, industry positioning, financial analysis, and a multi-method valuation framework.

The report concludes with an intrinsic value estimate of **$81.6/share** (DCF – Perpetuity Growth) against a market price of **$122.8**, implying a ~50% premium reflecting investor confidence in Walmart's ongoing business transformation.
